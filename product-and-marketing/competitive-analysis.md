## Market Overview

Developer newsletters are a crowded space, but AI-coding-specific newsletters are a fast-growing niche with few established players. The primary competition comes from: (1) general developer newsletters covering AI as one topic, (2) general AI newsletters not focused on development, and (3) other "vibe coding" or AI-dev-focused newsletters.

## Positioning Relative to Market

VibeCoding Newsletter occupies a specific niche: AI-native developer workflows. It is narrower than general AI newsletters (Ben's Bites) but deeper. It is more practical than general dev newsletters (Pragmatic Engineer) and more AI-focused. The connection to the EndOfCoding content ecosystem creates a distribution and credibility moat that standalone newsletters cannot replicate.
