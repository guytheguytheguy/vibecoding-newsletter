## Mission

VibeCoding Newsletter exists to help developers stay ahead of the AI coding revolution. Every week, a curated, practical edition lands in subscribers' inboxes: tool deep dives, vibe coding workflows, industry trends, and direct links to the best resources. Free. Always.

---

## Value Propositions

### 1. Curated, Not Aggregated
Every edition is hand-selected — not scraped or algorithmically generated. The signal-to-noise ratio is high because we only cover what actually matters to developers building with AI.

### 2. Practical, Not Theoretical
Every issue contains something actionable: a workflow, a prompt pattern, a tool breakdown, a step-by-step tutorial. Readers can immediately apply what they learn.

### 3. Connected to the Ecosystem
VibeCoding Newsletter is the content layer of the EndOfCoding ecosystem — including the EndOfCoding YouTube channel, Vibe Coding Academy, and the Vibe Coding eBook. Subscribers get first access to new content, course discounts, and community announcements.
